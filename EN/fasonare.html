About the Factory

Our rebar processing factory is equipped with modern machinery for cutting, bending, and shaping steel reinforcement. We provide fast, precise services with direct delivery to site.

B-Masiv integrates high quality standards, material traceability, and clear execution timelines in every production flow. We manage orders for residential, commercial, and industrial projects with technical support from quotation to delivery.

We work with specialized teams, advanced machinery, and stage-by-stage quality checks so every order is prepared correctly, efficiently, and safely.
